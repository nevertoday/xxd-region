You want the US App Store. You open **Settings**, tap your name, tap **Media & Purchases**, and there it is: **Country/Region**. Tap it, pick United States, and it looks like a two-minute job, far less hassle than making a whole new account.

But the first line of Apple's article on changing region says that before you update your location, you should spend your remaining Apple Account balance, and you must also cancel any subscriptions that block a country or region change ([Change your Apple Account country or region](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118283)). Read on, and there's a whole list of things to clear.

## What you have to clear first

Take the main account you've used for years and check it against Apple's list ([Change your Apple Account country or region](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118283)):

- **Spend the balance.** If there's an <Term k="Apple 账户余额" /> left, spend it; if what's left is less than the cost of a single item, contact Apple Support to clear it. You can't turn the balance into cash, except as required by law.
- **Cancel subscriptions, then wait.** Cancel any subscriptions that block a change, then wait until the end of the subscription period. If you cancel iCloud+ or Apple One, your iCloud storage is reduced to the free 5 GB plan. If you have a lot of photos, note what that means: when your storage exceeds your plan, iCloud won't sync or update your information, and iCloud backups won't complete ([Downgrade or cancel your iCloud+ plan](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108318)).
- **Wait for pending items.** Memberships, pre-orders, movie rentals and Season Passes have to complete, and any pending refunds from Apple have to process.
- **Check <Term k="家人共享" />.** If you're part of a Family Sharing group, you might not be able to change.
- **Have a payment method for the new region ready.** You might need to enter a valid payment method for the new country or region. If you don't want to, Apple's advice is to wait until you're physically there and then create a new Apple Account.
- **Download your content first.** Some content might not be available in the new country or region, so Apple suggests redownloading your apps, music, movies, TV shows and books on every device you'll use them on.

One more applies only to China mainland accounts. iCloud in China mainland is operated by <Term k="云上贵州" /> ([Learn more about iCloud in China mainland](https://support.apple.com/en-us/111754)). Under the terms for iCloud operated by GCBD, moving an account from China mainland to another region means you expressly consent to your iCloud content being transferred across the border to Apple, outside China mainland, and "Upon completion of the migration of your Content in iCloud, GCBD will delete the relevant Content and no longer provide the Service." ([iCloud operated by GCBD terms and conditions](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/icloud/en/gcbd-terms.html)) The Chinese version of Apple's change-region article has a section saying the same ([118283, Chinese version](https://support.apple.com/zh-cn/118283)); the English version doesn't. Your photos, contacts and backups all live in that one iCloud.

<Fig id="l3-before-change" />

Each item makes sense on its own. Added up, though, it means emptying your main account and then waiting a while, and for a China mainland account, moving your whole iCloud out as well. And what happens to things you bought before isn't clear either: the article only says some content might not be available in the new region, so download it first; it doesn't say whether your purchase history stays or goes. Apple's pages don't say; if you're unsure, ask Apple Support first.

<mark><strong>Moving a main account you've used for years means clearing the balance, subscriptions, pending items and Family Sharing first, and a China mainland account's iCloud moves out of GCBD too. For one app, a second account is far simpler.</strong></mark>

## Make a second account, and leave your main one alone

With a second account, none of the above applies. Your main account stays in China mainland with its balance, subscriptions, Family Sharing and iCloud exactly as they are. You pick the United States or Türkiye when you create the new account, then sign it in under **Media & Purchases** only, as [lesson 2](/en/start/2-two-sign-ins/) showed. That's the setup Apple itself describes: one account for iCloud and most features on your phone, another used just for purchases ([Migrate purchases from one Apple Account to another Apple Account](https://support.apple.com/en-us/117294)).

Apple describes how to create an account in [How to create a new Apple Account](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108647): you can do it on the web or on an iPhone, iPad or Mac, and along the way you verify your email address, create a strong password and set the country or region. The country or region you choose becomes the account's <Term k="居住国家或地区" />. [Lesson 1](/en/start/1-region/) covered what the terms say about that, so think it over before you choose.

For the sign-up itself, step by step, including which payment option to pick, the full guide takes you through [step 1](/en/guide/1-prepare/) to [step 3](/en/guide/3-payment/). Or go straight to [Create a US Apple Account](/en/howto/us-account/) or [Create a Turkey Apple Account](/en/howto/tr-account/).

## That's the three lessons

You now know the three things a beginner needs: an account's region decides which store you walk into, and the terms treat it as where you live; iCloud always stays on your own account, and the other-region account goes only under Media & Purchases; and if you want an account in another region, you make a second one and leave your main account alone.

## If something's off

<Q q="I still want to move my whole main account to the US.">

Then work through the list above one item at a time; [Move an existing account to another region](/en/howto/change-region/) has the details. Before you start, think again about the point from lesson 1: the terms treat an account's region as where you live. For a China mainland account, count the iCloud move out of GCBD as well.

</Q>
<Q q="It keeps saying I can't change my region.">

Apple says to make sure you've cancelled your subscriptions, waited for pending activity to clear and spent your account balance; if your balance is less than the cost of a single item, contact Apple Support to clear it ([Change your Apple Account country or region](https://support.apple.com/en-us/118283)).

</Q>
<Q q="I want to move the apps my main account bought over to the new account.">

Apple does offer purchase migration, but it requires both accounts to be set to the same country and region, and after a migration the secondary account can no longer be used for Media & Purchases ([Migrate purchases from one Apple Account to another Apple Account](https://support.apple.com/en-us/117294)). A China mainland main account and a new US account are in different regions, so by that rule they can't be merged. The apps your main account bought stay with it; when you want to download or update them, switch Media & Purchases back to your main account.

</Q>
<Q q="I'm in a Family Sharing group and want to add the new account to it.">

Apple's terms say: "All Family members must share the same Home Country." The China mainland terms say the same ([US terms](https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/us/terms.html), [China mainland terms](https://www.apple.com.cn/legal/internet-services/itunes/cn/terms.html), in Chinese). So a new US or Turkey account and your China mainland family group don't fit together.

</Q>
<Q q="Signing up the new account says “Your account cannot be created at this time.”">

Apple has an article on exactly this message: try another way to create the account, in **Settings** or **System Settings** if you have an Apple device, or at account.apple.com if you're on a Windows PC or Android device, and update to the latest software ([If you can’t create an Apple Account or see “your account cannot be created at this time”](https://support.apple.com/en-us/120636)). If that doesn't help, see ["Your account cannot be created at this time"](/en/howto/cannot-create/).

</Q>
